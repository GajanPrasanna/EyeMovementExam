Live front camera

Sit about 40–60 cm from the tablet. Keep your head facing forward, use even lighting, and follow the red target with your eyes. The preview is mirrored for convenience.

How to use this prototype

  1. Tap Start camera and grant camera permission.
  2. When the face model has loaded, tap Calibrate gaze. Look at each red target and keep your head still.
  3. After calibration, tap Run eye-movement task and follow the moving red target.
  4. Review the tracking report at the bottom. Large differences mean the tracking needs review; they do not prove a medical defect.

Privacy: this page does not save video or send camera frames to a server. The face-landmark model files and software are downloaded from Google/CDN servers. Close the camera when finished.

Important limitations

This first version estimates gaze from iris position relative to visible eye landmarks. It is not calibrated in degrees, has not been validated against a clinical eye-movement examination, and may miss or falsely flag abnormalities. It cannot determine the cause of an abnormal movement or localise a lesion to a specific cranial nerve. A real assessment requires a trained clinician and an appropriate examination.
